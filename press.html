Fact sheet

The short version

What it is

Me. is a query-first iOS app that reads the Apple Health data you already have, lets you add your own context, and compiles a structured document ready for whoever you choose to read it: any GP, any coach, or any AI (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini). Me. does not tell you what your results mean. It compiles the structure that makes real interpretation possible.

Works with

Any device that writes to Apple Health.

Privacy

Everything stays on your device. No Me. account. No Me. server. Optional iCloud backup, controlled in settings. TelemetryDeck anonymous, privacy-first analytics, disclosed in-app with an opt-out toggle.

Pricing

Free tier is full: check-ins, notes, complete profile, saved prompts, training plans, and 7-day compiles up to 5 times a month. Me. Pro adds full-history compilation, unlimited compiles, and profile change history, with a 7-day free trial. USD $2.99 a month, or USD $19.99 once. No subscription required: unlock Me. Pro once and own it. Launching internationally.

Founder Q&A

From the founder

Selected answers; full Q&A available on request.
Why did you build this?
Turning 40, I could have bought a motorbike. Instead, I built a health app. It may be the most productive midlife crisis I could have chosen. Me. came from about three years of trying to connect everything I was learning about health and training with the data I was already collecting. I was good at recording things: workouts, sleep, health metrics. The problem was that the data and the learning were disconnected. The data sat across Apple Health and a handful of other apps, each showing part of the picture, sometimes contradicting each other. The problem is not collection. It is context. Me. became the support system underneath those decisions. And that gap is not just mine. Most people already have the data. They just cannot use it properly.
Why not just use the native Apple Health connector in ChatGPT?
Use it if it works for you. Access to metrics is not the same as knowing the person. My watch knew I slept badly. It had no idea a baby was involved. Me. adds the information surrounding the numbers: your context, your conditions and medications, your notes about how things felt and what was happening in your life, compiled with the data into one structured document you can check before you hand it over. And Me. prepares that record for use beyond any one provider or conversation. Platforms change. Features close. A person should not lose their health context because one piece of infrastructure changes. The platforms connect your data. Me. prepares it, and keeps it yours.
What about privacy?
You are not trusting us with your health data. We are giving you a place to hold it yourself. We cannot access the health data stored in Me. It stays on your device. It cannot be sold because we do not have it. There is no Me. account and no Me. server. If you want a backup, optional iCloud backup is there, controlled entirely from your own settings. Me. uses TelemetryDeck for anonymous, privacy-first analytics, disclosed in-app with an opt-out toggle. And if Oi stopped operating tomorrow, your data would still be on your device. The record remains yours.
Why one-time pricing?
Because your health data is not something you should have to keep paying to keep using. Me. is free to use fully: check-ins, notes, your complete profile, saved prompts, training plans, and compiling the last 7 days up to 5 times a month. Me. Pro adds full-history compilation, unlimited compiles, and profile change history, with a 7-day free trial. After that, USD $2.99 a month if you would rather pay as you go, or USD $19.99 once. No subscription required: unlock Me. Pro once and own it. You pay once, and you have it. You can change providers, doctors, coaches, or AI tools without rebuilding your entire history.
